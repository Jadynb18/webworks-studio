# 3. Target Users & User Needs

## Maya — Mobile Regular

**Context:**  
Maya lives in the area and visits Hill Country Coffee several times each month. She usually accesses the website from her phone while she is already out.

**Primary goal:**  
Quickly find café hours, location information, and available drinks or food options.
**Important information/tasks:**  
Check opening hours, find the nearest café information, and see whether familiar menu items are available.
**Likely friction:**  
Having to search through multiple pages, unclear navigation labels, buried hours, and menus that are difficult to scan on a phone.
**Design/accessibility implication:**  
The website should have clear navigation, readable text, touch-friendly controls, and information that is easy to scan on mobile devices.
## Daniel — Hill Country Visitor
**Context:**  
Daniel is visiting Central Texas and searches online for local coffee shops while planning a day trip. He has never visited Hill Country Coffee before.
**Primary goal:**  
Learn about Hill Country Coffee and decide whether it is a good fit for his plans.
**Important information/tasks:**  
Understand the business, explore the menu, find café locations, and review photographs and descriptions.
**Likely friction:**  
Unclear navigation, missing location details, information that assumes visitors already know the business, and important details only available in images.
**Design/accessibility implication:**  
The website should use recognizable navigation labels, provide clear location information, and make important content available as readable text.
## Renee — Deliberate Planner

**Context:**  
Renee enjoys meeting friends at local cafés and prefers to review her options before choosing a location. She uses both desktop and mobile devices.
**Primary goal:**  
Review menu choices and business information so she can plan a visit with friends.
**Important information/tasks:**  
Review menu categories, identify food and beverage choices, understand the business atmosphere and values, and share information with friends.
**Likely friction:**  
Small text, low contrast, layouts that break when text is enlarged, and menus that rely on PDFs or images.
**Design/accessibility implication:**  
The website should have readable text, good color contrast, flexible layouts, meaningful headings, and descriptive links.
